# Business, tenant and subscription lifecycle standards

Status: proposed for owner review, October 18, 2026 Pacific, as an owner-requested extension of PR #61. Documentation only; no code, service, migration, dependency, infrastructure, audit, issue-creation, deployment or merge authority. Use [operational readiness](operational-readiness.md), [security](saas-security.md), [platform rules](talix-platform.md), [integrations](integrations.md), [accessibility](accessibility.md) and [Definition of Done](definition-of-done.md). The focused implemented/partial/future assessment is owned by [the operational assessment](operational-readiness.md#focused-assessment-actual-capability-and-limits), not duplicated here. These standards preserve client → subscription → location ownership, shared/location roles and immutable purchase/history semantics. No new lifecycle schema or state enum is mandated.

## B01 — Data ownership, classification and safe lifecycle (L1)

**Purpose/applicability:** Protect tenant data from creation through eventual disposal. **Requirement:** Identify owner/steward, tenant scope, sensitivity/classification, allowed uses/access, retention/archive criteria and justified exceptions. Define deletion/anonymization, accidental-deletion protection and restoration limits, including retained immutable records and backups. Resolve legal/contractual holds and provider restrictions through appropriate separate review; do not invent legal obligations or retention periods. **Verification:** Review authorized data-flow/retention matrix, cross-tenant denial and safe deletion/recovery procedures for affected data. **Timing:** New data categories or lifecycle changes; before any approved destructive/export operation. **Limits:** No live deletion, archival or export implementation. Subscription changes alone must not irreversibly erase data; preserve S06/S07/S13/I69.

## B02 — Portability and tenant offboarding (L2)

**Purpose/applicability:** Close or transition a tenant/account without hidden loss or continued access. **Requirement:** For an approved closure/export capability, define authorized requester/tenant scope, export opportunity, format/completeness, secure delivery/expiry, subscription termination, account deactivation, credentials/sessions/connection revocation, late events/pending work, retention/deletion/anonymization and confirmation/auditability. Account closure, one subscription cancellation and entire client offboarding are distinct. Explain backup retention and restoration/deletion limitations; disconnect does not prove provider deletion. **Verification:** Tenant-scoped export completeness/denial, safe delivery, offboarding/reconnect/recovery and retained-record evidence in an approved isolated scope. **Timing:** Before introducing or relying on offboarding/export; review lifecycle dependencies when affected. **Limits:** No current self-service export/closure promise, blanket account deletion, remote provider deletion or automatic purge. Core privacy/authority safeguards in B01 remain L1.

## B03 — Secure, repeat-safe tenant provisioning (L1)

**Purpose/applicability:** Establish ownership/isolation from the first provisioning operation. **Requirement:** Server-validate actual tenant/client/subscription identity, initial owner authority, unique ownership rules and cross-resource relationships. Apply approved initial roles/settings/defaults and subscription selections atomically as appropriate. Make retries safe, prevent duplicate provisioning, preserve existing shared client details and immutable additional-subscription purchases. Treat saved onboarding metadata as untrusted input; never derive authority/prices from it. **Verification:** Owner/outsider/unverified identity cases as applicable, cross-tenant references, duplicate request/changed payload, interrupted/partial failure and additional-purchase preservation. Real concurrency evidence when lock/race behavior changes. **Timing:** New/changed provisioning boundaries and acceptance. **Limits:** Current checked SQL and development email exception remain; no ownership proof or entitlement implementation beyond actual source is claimed. No new provisioning flow/schema authorized.

## B04 — Ownership and business setup recovery (L2)

**Purpose/applicability:** Make approved onboarding/role/setup flows clear and recoverable. **Requirement:** Define setup status, incomplete-state recovery, safe resume/retry and initial configuration. New invitation, role/ownership-transfer and recovery features need explicit identity, approval, audit and fail-safe authority rules; do not assume current account ownership transfer/invitations exist. Preserve existing-website / Talix-primary-template / decide-later direction, storefront defaults, configuration-driven customization and industry boundaries. Included adjustable templates should not silently incur an extra recurring website charge; custom website work may have a separately approved design fee. **Verification:** Appropriate keyboard/screen-reader/device flow, abandoned/restarted setup, lost-response retry, denied access and safe fallback without overwriting existing business details. **Timing:** Next approved owner journey proposal and affected implementation acceptance. **Limits:** No fixed placement/templates/domain/hosting/fee or new multi-owner model selected. Current tenant authority remains B03/S01/S02 L1; setup/ownership features require their own approved scope.

## B05 — Subscription transitions and authoritative eligibility (L1)

**Purpose/applicability:** Keep actual subscription-restricted capabilities consistent across all clients/entry points. **Requirement:** Document only supported states/transitions and their source of truth: identity, permitted actor, eligibility effect, timing, history/version, retry and stale-state rules. Evaluate pending/trial/active/past-due/suspended/canceled/expired and activation/conversion/upgrade/downgrade/renewal/cancellation/reactivation/expiry/payment-failure/grace periods only if included in the approved lifecycle. Centrally enforce actual entitlements server-side, including APIs/jobs/events where applicable; never fork plan-name authority in each UI/provider. **Verification:** Transition table and negative tenant/industry/tier/revoked/stale/direct-call tests; preserve immutable original purchase and checked change history. **Timing:** With actual restricted features/lifecycle changes, not before a hypothetical engine exists. **Limits:** Existing generic catalog and Owner industry changes are not a complete billing/entitlement lifecycle. No new state model, grace period, pricing, engine or live billing activated here.

## B06 — Billing reliability and entitlement data protection (L1)

**Purpose/applicability:** Prevent duplicate effects and irreversible loss when subscription terms change. **Requirement:** For approved billing-related operations, define transaction/event authenticity, idempotency, ordering, safe retry/unknown outcomes, reconciliation and audit/history. State access restriction versus retention/export/restoration behavior after entitlement loss; do not automatically destroy stored data on downgrade/cancellation. Protect pending reconciliation and provider lifecycle operations under reviewed authority. **Verification:** Duplicate/replayed/changed request, stale/reordered event, authorization, partial/unknown success, failed renewal and recovery tests where actually applicable; verify preserved data/history and no duplicate transaction. **Timing:** Existing affected change transactions and any later approved billing integration. **Limits:** Shopper payment simulation and sample subscription prices remain unchanged; no real payment/provider/event or subscription mutation executed. Reuse S08/I61/I62/I67 and separately approved billing requirements, not invented financial rules.

## B07 — Privileged access and support governance (L1)

**Purpose/applicability:** Keep administrative operations within explicit authority. **Requirement:** Least privilege, effective role/resource checks, protected Owner and separate Talix staff authority; defined approval/escalation and separation of duties where justified. Support access needs explicit authorization, tenant scope, accountable actor and time limits where warranted. Never self-grant elevated power from user metadata or ordinary client requests. **Verification:** Owner/staff/manager/outsider/revoked-access denial cases; review privileged operation scope and credential access. **Timing:** New/changed privileged/support workflows and acceptance. **Limits:** No support impersonation, override, time-bound-access service or new admin framework introduced. Advanced support tooling is L3 future guidance only after approval.

## B08 — Sensitive action auditability (L2)

**Purpose/applicability:** Attribute and investigate meaningful privileged/data actions. **Requirement:** Determine risk-based audit coverage for ownership, roles/permissions, subscriptions, sensitive settings, integration credentials, overrides and critical export/delete/recovery operations. Record actor, actual authority/tenant/resource, action/time, safe correlation/result and necessary change context. Limit access, minimize secrets/payloads, define retention and tamper-resistance proportional to risk. Keep audit history distinct from debug logs and immutable order/purchase snapshots. **Verification:** Review attribution/completeness, denied/tampered/read-access cases and actual retention/integrity evidence for implemented records. **Timing:** Material sensitive operations as introduced/changed; coverage gap is explicit, not presumed solved by generic logging. **Limits:** No universal audit stream, new table/provider or secret-in-history requirement. L1 access/data safeguards remain B01/B07/S rules; advanced audit infrastructure is optional and separately approved.

## Review boundaries

B01/B03/B05/B06/B07 are L1; B02/B04/B08 are L2. Unimplemented native/advanced administrative/offline/industry features remain future guidance in their owning X/P standards, not lifecycle implementation requirements. Exact retention, ownership-transfer policy, closure/export scope, trial/payment/grace rules, capability mapping, support authority and audit coverage need concrete approved requirements and evidence. No conflicts with approved architecture identified; no issue, onboarding/billing implementation, data changes, migrations or service actions are authorized by this document.
