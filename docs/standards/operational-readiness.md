## Principle and scope

**Talix must be engineered so that business-critical operations remain secure, observable, recoverable, and maintainable throughout the lifecycle of each tenant, subscription, deployment, and platform feature.**

Use [AGENTS](../../AGENTS.md), [architecture](../ARCHITECTURE.md), [security S01–S14](saas-security.md), [workflow W01–W06](testing-and-workflow.md), [platform P01–P09](talix-platform.md), [cross-platform](cross-platform.md), [accessibility](accessibility.md), [integrations](integrations.md), [business lifecycle](business-lifecycle.md) and [Definition of Done](definition-of-done.md). These standards extend existing authorities rather than establish a competing release process or architecture. Safeguards scale with actual operational risk; advanced infrastructure is not a prerequisite to ordinary development.

## O01 — Structured, tenant-safe diagnostics (L1)

**Purpose/applicability:** Make material current operations diagnosable without disclosing protected data. **Requirement:** Record safe operation/correlation identifiers, outcome, meaningful severity/error class and enough context for authorized investigation; actionable customer language must distinguish loading, empty, unavailable, error and unknown outcome. Redact secrets/session tokens/private links and unnecessary customer payloads. Scope tenant/operator access and log retention. **Verification:** Inspect redacted success/failure examples, unauthorized log access and correlation through the affected operation. **Timing:** New/materially changed critical operations, before affected acceptance. **Limits:** S04/S12/I64 remain governing; no logging provider or new centralized system required merely by prose.

## O02 — Monitoring, alerts and incidents (L2)

**Purpose/applicability:** Detect and recover from actual operational failures proportionately. **Requirement:** Define coverage for availability, critical business journeys, API/database/Auth and relevant integration/background failures and performance. Alerts need impact-based severity, action, responsible owner and escalation; reduce duplicates/noise. An incident record should capture severity/blast radius, timeline, investigation evidence, safe containment, recovery verification, authorized communications, root cause and prioritized corrective actions. Protect tenant information in tools/communications and record notification responsibilities without exposing incident secrets. **Verification:** Review runbook/ownership and, in separately approved safe exercises, demonstrate alert-to-owner and containment/recovery steps. **Timing:** Before relying on a production critical journey, and after material operational changes/incidents. **Limits:** No vendor, numeric availability guarantee, on-call staffing promise or communication sending authorized. Critical diagnostic secrecy/correctness remains L1 through O01/S rules. Enterprise telemetry/automated response is L3 future guidance only when justified.

## O03 — Backup and recovery safeguards (L1)

**Purpose/applicability:** Recover business-critical data and relationships safely. **Requirement:** Assign backup/recovery owners and inventory scope: database/schema/history, Auth identity, Storage objects/metadata, needed configuration/secrets references and external dependencies. Define approved risk-based frequency/retention, secure storage/access, accidental-deletion protection, restoration limitations and tenant-integrity validation. Set RPO/RTO through approved business requirements, recording assumptions and measured evidence; no numeric guarantees invented here. **Verification:** Review actual coverage/configuration and a documented, successfully verified isolated restore procedure, including ownership/RLS, histories, assets and any excluded items. Backup creation alone is insufficient. **Timing:** Feasible recovery review before risky operational changes (S13), and verified strategy before production-critical reliance. **Limits:** No backup configuration, creation, download or restore executed here. Code rollback does not restore data, credentials or provider effects; unverified backup claims must remain unverified.

## O04 — Business continuity and recovery exercises (L2)

**Purpose/applicability:** Make recovery actionable during credible outages. **Requirement:** Document failure scenarios, dependencies, authorized responders/access, containment, recovery order, safe reconciliation of external effects, service validation and business communication/escalation. Rehearse restoration against approved objectives and record measured RPO/RTO results, gaps, accountable follow-up and review dates after material changes. Protect other tenants during tenant-scoped recovery; shared restore cannot silently overwrite unrelated data. **Verification:** Approved isolated exercise evidence and reconciliation/operational acceptance record. **Timing:** Production planning and material recovery changes; exercises at an approved risk-based cadence. **Limits:** No drill, guaranteed recovery time, multi-region design or enterprise continuity system mandated now. Multi-region/automated disaster recovery is L3 future guidance; underlying recovery safety remains O03 L1.

## O05 — Release and migration safety (L1)

**Purpose/applicability:** Activate only reviewed compatible changes in the intended environment. **Requirement:** Record exact reviewed commit, environment and service/schema compatibility, required tests/review/owner acceptance and release notes. Keep implementation, migration execution, infrastructure, merge and deployment authorizations distinct. Preserve immutable applied migrations and existing disabled main deployment. Plan backward-compatible API/database/client/integration transitions/deprecations and pre/post-release verification. **Verification:** Exact diff/CI evidence, environment checks and separately authorized execution record; verify actual post-release critical behavior and recovery feasibility. **Timing:** Each applicable merge/activation/release under existing W02/S07/S13. **Limits:** No workflow/settings changes or migration execution authorized; a successful build/READY preview is not release acceptance or live verification.

## O06 — Rollout, rollback and failed release recovery (L2)

**Purpose/applicability:** Limit impact and recover safely from material releases. **Requirement:** Define rollout population/tenant eligibility, activation/deactivation, observation/stop conditions, release owner and compatible fallback/forward fix. Use flags only when justified; flags are not authorization gates or substitutes for entitlements. Before rollback, verify old code fits current data/schema/API/credentials/provider state. Escalate as an incident if rollback is unsafe; verify recovery afterward and reconcile effects. **Verification:** Review concrete activation/recovery scenarios, tenant boundaries, stale clients and planned critical-flow checks. **Timing:** Material rollout planning and operational review. **Limits:** No universal flag service or flag for each small change. Destructive reversal requires explicit approved data/recovery plan; no replay/rewrite of migration history. Required release safety remains O05 L1; advanced traffic management is L3 optional guidance.

## O07 — Evidence-based performance objectives (L2)

**Purpose/applicability:** Bound costly operations and avoid regressions in demonstrated workflows. **Requirement:** Establish objectives from approved business/device/network/volume needs; record metric, dataset, environment, tool/method, baseline and acceptable regression/limit. Review queries/indexes, bounded lists/pagination, API payload/request counts, rendering/assets and any justified caching/background processing; preserve tenant/privacy/transaction correctness. **Verification:** Reproducible representative timings/query plans/profiles where relevant, with actual result/limit disclosure and affected regression checks. **Timing:** Resource-intensive or performance-sensitive changes and capacity review. **Limits:** No invented latency/throughput/SLA, caching/queue service or benchmark number. Use S14/X47/I66; ordinary documentation changes do not require profiling.

## O08 — Capacity and multi-tenant workload protection (L2)

**Purpose/applicability:** Prevent resource growth or abusive work from harming other tenants. **Requirement:** Track relevant utilization, storage growth, concurrency, quotas and saturation for actual workloads; set justified bounded requests/batches and tenant fairness/limits where appropriate. Isolate expensive work using approved existing mechanisms before scaling architecture. Plan capacity based on measured demand, not hypothetical size. **Verification:** Approved isolated load/concurrency scenarios and noisy-tenant cases, request/batch bounds and resource/headroom observations. **Timing:** Before materially increasing workloads or production capacity reliance. **Limits:** No shared-production load tests, new queue/cache/autoscaling or telemetry configuration authorized. Advanced autoscaling is L3 future guidance. L1 denial/privacy/data-integrity obligations still apply under S/I rules.

## Review and unresolved decisions

O01/O03/O05 are mandatory safeguards; O02/O04/O06/O07/O08 strongly enforced operational practices. Embedded L3 advanced options are not immediate implementation. Existing classification/exception rules apply; requirements unavailable for verification need explicit review, not fabricated passing evidence.

Outstanding decisions include actual recovery/retention owners and objectives, production incident/escalation/communication responsibilities, verification environments, workload measurements/capacity objectives and exact release/recovery plans. No architectural conflict identified. Scope these before operational reliance; this proposal does not buy/configure tools or silently declare production ready.
